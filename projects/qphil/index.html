QPHIL: Q-learning with Prioritized Hindsight for Imitation Learning

Mathieu Petitbois, et al.

← Back to Main Site

Overview

QPHIL is a novel method that integrates Q-learning with prioritized hindsight to boost performance in long-horizon imitation learning environments. It is robust to sparse rewards and generalizes across diverse start and goal configurations.
