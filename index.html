About Me

Welcome to my personal website! I am a PhD Student currently working at Ubisoft La Forge and LERIA advised by Ludovic Denoyer, Sylvain Lamprier and Rémy Portelas. My research interests include reinforcement learning, imitation learning and diverse behavior learning. More specifically, I am interested in the learning of diverse and controlable agents capable of displaying a high variety of behaviors. I am also passionate about applying AI to solve challenging problems in simulated (videogames) and real (robotics) worlds.
